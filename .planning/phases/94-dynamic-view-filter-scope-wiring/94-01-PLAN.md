<objective>
Extend the combination orchestrator to OWN dv-bound visualizations (widgets + map layers), and flip the two dv-bound renderer read-paths (AggregatedWidgetRenderer + MapChartRenderer) from the legacy `filterViewStore.dvViews` slice to `filterCombinationStore`. This is the exact same discipline Phase 91/92 applied to the table path, now applied to the dv path: the orchestrator becomes the sole materialize trigger and renderers become read-only consumers of the combination store.

This closes FSCOPE-V118-03's engine half (the env-flag/UI half is Plan 02). dv-bound vizs now apply their `filterSelection` allow-list against `dvFilters[dvId]` (NOT the base-table filters), keyed by `stableComboHash("dv", dvId, resolved)`.

Purpose: dv-backed widgets/layers get per-visualization filter selection, deduped + ref-counted identically to the table path, with NO dual-trigger and NO new SQLite storage.
Output: orchestrator dv enumeration; legacy WidgetRenderer dv-branch removed; both dv renderer read-paths on the combo store; orchestrator spec covering the three source-type cases.
</objective>
