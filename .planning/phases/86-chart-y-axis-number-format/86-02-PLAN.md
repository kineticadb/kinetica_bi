Wire the renderer side of the hybrid Y-axis formatter (AXIS-V117-02 resolution) and apply it to the Y-axis TICK labels only (AXIS-V117-03) on BOTH `TimelineRenderer` and `NumericLineRenderer`.

- Extended renderer specs proving override + column-default fallback + tooltip isolation.
</objective>
