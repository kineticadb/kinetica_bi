<objective>
Define the serializable widget-action contract and the versioned, allow-list-based validation that is the AI-safety boundary for the entire engine. This is the foundation every downstream caller (the radio widget in Phase 59/60, a future AI/MCP layer) is bound by.

Purpose: ENGINE-V111-01 (serializable zod-validated envelope, the single shape every caller produces) and SAFETY-V111-01 (versioned allow-list rejecting out-of-list / meta / proto keys — no free-form Object.assign). Per research (PITFALLS P6 / TENSION 3), the allow-list MUST ship in the foundation — retrofitting after control widgets exist is a breaking change.

Output: `zod@^3` added to packages/web; `lib/widgetAction.ts` (envelope + schema + typed result), `lib/actionAllowList.ts` (versioned allow-list + validator), and their spec files. NO server changes.
