<objective>
Create the per-user, in-memory session store (turns + cursor + derived) that drives everything else, the hooks the read sites and edit-lock use, and the scripted-op test harness.

Design (134-RESEARCH Pattern 1/2, recorded deviation from PITFALLS C7 — see 134-02): state = `{ dashboardId, baseEntries, droppedCount, turns, cursor, nextTempId, geometry, derived, pendingLeave }`. `derived = foldSession(baseline, [...baseEntries, ...turns.slice(0, cursor+1).flatMap(t => t.entries)])`, recomputed in the SAME `set()` that moves the cursor, then the AI contribution is rebuilt from the whole log prefix and written once under `AI_SOURCE_ID` (never per turn — that is the source-keyed replace hazard). Cap (D-06): squash the oldest turn into `baseEntries` (do NOT `shift()`; later turns depend on earlier ops).

Locked semantics: D-02 revert does not delete later turns; D-03 a new turn while `cursor < turns.length-1` drops later turns; D-04 Discard/Keep clear, reload clears (no persistence of any kind); D-05 one history per dashboard per user (a different `baseline.dashboardId` resets first); D-17 op-less turns do not lock; D-11 every AI rebuild (new turn, revert, forward) is the AI's "most recent" write.

Purpose: AISES-V20-01/02/03/07 at the store level.
Output: store, three hooks, harness, specs; decoupling spec extended.
