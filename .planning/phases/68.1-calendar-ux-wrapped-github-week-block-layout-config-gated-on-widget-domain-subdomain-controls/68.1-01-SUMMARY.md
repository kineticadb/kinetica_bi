## Decisions Made

- **day subdomain rows=7 always:** GitHub-style — the block always has 7 DOW slots even if data doesn't cover all 7; consistent block height simplifies Plan 03 renderer.
- **hour subdomain cols=6:** 6 divides 24 evenly into 4 rows; a cleaner 6×4 grid than 8×3 or 4×6.
- **week subdomain cols=ceil(sqrt(count)) cap 13:** adaptive compact grid for variable-length week ranges (e.g. 52 weeks of a year → 8×7); cap at 13 prevents ultra-wide blocks.
- **month subdomain cols=4:** standard 4×3 grid for 12 months — matches calendar-year intuition.
- **parseUTCMs normalizer:** Kinetica DATE_TRUNC emits `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm:ss"` (space, no Z); `new Date("YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm:ss")` is implementation-defined (local vs UTC). Normalize to `"YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ssZ"` before parsing — safe UTC regardless of runtime.
