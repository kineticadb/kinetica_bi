<objective>
Hide the SHAPE* style fields (SHAPEFILLCOLOR / SHAPELINECOLOR / SHAPELINEWIDTH and their alpha controls) in the config UI when a layer's spatialMode is `latlon` — in BOTH the layer-level raster params group (`KineticaWmsLayerForm`) and the per-break advanced panel (`CbConfigForm`). SHAPE* params style polygon/line geometry and do nothing for point layers, so showing them confuses operators.

Purpose: SHAPE-V114-01 (layer-level raster) + SHAPE-V114-02 (per-break cb raster). Mirrors the v1.9 Phase 53 `spatialMode !== "track"` raster gate + `trackContext` prop precedent — this is the analogous `latlon` gate for the SHAPE* subset only. Point styling (POINT*) and Antialiasing stay visible because points legitimately use them.

Output: Two component edits + two spec-file extensions. UI-only — emission suppression (the leak fix) is Plan 02's concern.
</objective>
